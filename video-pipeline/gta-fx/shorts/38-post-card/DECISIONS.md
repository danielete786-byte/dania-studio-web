## v2 concept
- **The one idea: the post becomes the headline.** A faithful X post lands as a full-safe-width object (864 px, x 72–936) over a cut montage. Its key phrase is highlighted, and on the payoff cut that phrase **lifts off the card and flies up into the headline slot** in one FLIP move. The big type is the hook; the card underneath is the receipt.
- **Shots (r09 for v0, r08 for v1).** v0, daylight into sunset, sharp, with a light grade (contrast 1.08, saturation 1.12): `s011` Vice highway from its first frame, dropped 170 px and scaled 1.06 from its bottom edge (0–1.75; the camera cranes out of the windshield, the towers and signs sit behind the card, the pickup clears its lower edge from ~1.0 s) → **cut at 1.75 (payoff)** to `s100` biplane from media 0.35 (1.75–3.15: the towers and the pink smoke behind the headline, the wing walker right of it) → `s027` sunset dock (3.15–4.95, the native vertical shot with its own 1.0 → 1.05 push) → `s074` "Welcome to The Keys" (4.95–5.75, a quick cut) → **cut on the exit (5.75)** to `s064` jetski (5.75–6.5): the loop ends on the brightest, sharpest shot. v1, night into dusk, behind a focus pull: `s039` blue club close-up (0–1.6, from media 1.1, after the file's own cut from the wide floor at 1.07; sharp, then racks to a 5 px blur as the card racks *into* focus) → **cut at 1.6 (payoff)** to `s098` Jason with the fireworks (stops at media 1.1: the file cuts to a green shot at 1.13) → `s123` night drive (2.7–3.55, the lit Vice City skyline racked to ~8 px bokeh behind the numeral, the oncoming headlights under the card) → `s026` sunset pier, horizontal master on its right side (3.55–5.0: trees with fairy lights over the numeral, the posts and lit water under the card, no people) → `s027` sunset dock (5.0–6.5; racks back to sharp on the exit). The piece is 6.5 s.
- **Where the type sits.** Everything hangs on the x 72 safe line, left-aligned. The headline is in the upper third (y ≈ 300–580): in v0 that is the smoke, the water or the sky under a warm plum scrim; in v1 it is soft bokeh. The card sits under it (y ≈ 640–1262). Each v0 shot is framed so its subject lives in the gaps (above the headline, right of the card, or below it), never under the card.
- **Hero size and face.** v0: "PRE-LOAD / IS LIVE", Archivo wide 800 caps, fit 0.90 of the safe width (≈ 116 px), white with a pink-white bloom. v1: "90M+", Archivo wide 800, ≈ 222 px, sunset fill (echoed by the post's own "90M+" token once it lands). Both pulled left 0.035 em so the glyphs, the kicker and the card share the x 72 edge. Labels are 26–30 px wide-tracked caps; the card body is Inter 50 px (v0) / 46 px (v1). That is three tiers per frame.
- **The payoff hit.** The flight arrives on the cut, so the cut, light, shake, punch-zoom and RGB split land as it arrives. v0 at 1.75 s (since r09): the marked phrase splits into two chips, one per headline line, that peel off the card at 1.17 (leaving a gap in the post) and fly 1.43 → 1.75 (power2.in, building to 9 px of vertical motion blur), ~90 % home on the last frame before the cut; on the cut the complete white headline replaces them at their own width (Archivo's width axis) and stretches out to the wide face as a 1.08 slam settles, under `Look.expose` (no white flash), a 5 px RGB split, one warm leak and a warm sheen across the card; the phrase settles back into the post at P + 0.3. v1 at 1.6 s: the numeral itself starts as the post's own "90M+" token and flies to its slot reading 90M+ on every frame (since r08 there is no reel), lands on the cut 1.14× too big and settles in 0.4 s (expo.out) under the flash, the 14 px RGB split and the 1.04 punch-zoom; the card recoils, and the token settles back into the post in the sunset fill.

**Highlight and accent (one per frame).** v0 (Light card) keeps the sunset highlighter sweep inside the card, so its headline is white. v1 (Dark on blur) highlights the line the numeral comes from: it goes pure white (no bloom since r07) while the rest settles to 60 % white, and after the flight the line's own "90M+" sits in the same sunset fill as the numeral above it, so the source and the headline read as one accent. The avatar has no gradient ring. Since r06 the heart turns X pink, but only as the like *event* (the second beat), never as a static decoration (see round 6).
