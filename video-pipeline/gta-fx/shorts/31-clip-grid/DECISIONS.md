# Decisions: 31 Clip grid

## v2 concept (craft v2, round 6)
- **The one idea: the frame itself becomes the grid.** No backdrop, no cards, no chips. Every clip is full-bleed footage edge to edge, divided only by 6 px black seams, and the numbers sit on the image. v0 (2×2) builds the grid the way an editor would: each scene opens full frame, then **docks into its quarter** (the whole frame scales to 0.5 about its own corner) and reveals the next scene behind it. The 4th scene pulls back into the last cell, and the grid is complete. v1 (3 stacked) is a triptych: three full-width 16:9 bands **wipe in from alternating sides** on the beat, and at the end the outer bands wipe away so the middle band holds as a letterbox between the two lit seams.
- **Shots and cuts (all at 1.0x, each cell is one continuous shot).** v0: TL `s004` palm-shirt man by the pickup (0–4.25 s), TR `s007` Jason at the door (revealed 0.45), BL `s119` gameplay shootout (revealed 0.95, graded contrast 1.14 / saturate 1.14, zoomed 1.2 from the top so the gameplay reticle drops below the kicker), BR `s011` Vice City highway (revealed 1.45, pulls back 1.95–2.31, then takes the frame back 3.75–4.25 and the piece ends full-bleed on it: towers, green signs and the airliner, the brightest shot). Docks at 0.45 / 0.95 / 1.45 / 1.95 (0.36 s, expo.inOut, 5 px blur at mid-move). v1: `s014` the mural and police car (0 s, from the left, zoom 1.38 into the windscreen), `s008` Jason driving (0.4 s, from the right), `s012` beach gym (0.8 s, from the left); outer bands wipe off at 3.42 / 3.48; `s008` holds as the letterbox to 5.0.
- **Where the type sits and why.** Vertical shots put faces in the top third, so v0's numbers hug the horizontal seam: the top row's numbers sit at the bottom of their cells (shirt, torso: calm), the bottom row's hang from the top of theirs (street, sky: no faces). A soft shadow valley either side of the seam (panel-local, so it docks with the shot) plus a soft pool behind each number gives legibility without a box. Labels sit outward from the seam (above the top row, below the bottom row), so each pair of lockups mirrors across the seam. v1's numbers form one evenly spaced column at x 72 (01 on seam 1, 02 centred on band 2, 03 hanging from seam 2), each with a shadow pool from the left edge, the label beside it on the cap line. The kicker sits where the image is calm: v0 bottom-left of the BL cell, v1 top-left (in the black above the letterbox at the end).
- **Hero size and face.** v0: Archivo wide 800 numerals at 236 px (cap ≈ 160 px), labels Archivo wide 600 25 px tracked .3em, kicker 21 px. One family. v1: Inter display 800 numerals at 214 px ("01"–"03", tight −0.05em), labels and kicker Inter 600 27 px tracked .3em. One family. Three sizes per frame in both; numeral to label ≈ 8:1.
- **Payoff hit.** v0 at 2.29 s, when the 4th scene has pulled back and the grid is whole: `Look.impact` (flash 0, amp 7, zoom 1.028) + `Look.expose` (peak 1.55, 3-frame hold) on the footage, a warm flare at the seam cross (screen blend, 1-frame attack, 0.55 s decay) and the sunset seam line drawing out from the cross to both edges (0.6 s expo.out), which then rests at 88 %. v1 at 1.22 s, when band 3 has landed: impact + expose (peak 1.5), and both seams draw left to right in the sunset (0.6 s, the second 0.06 s later). The sunset is the one accent in both variants: it lives only in the seam light.

## Key decisions (v2)
- **v1's look is gone.** The rounded cards on an ink-navy backdrop, the two-part chips, the Barlow Condensed title, the segmented progress rule, the "2/4" count and the veil on the clips that don't lead were exactly what the owner called basic: a web dashboard of small tiles. They are deleted, not restyled. The job (several clips at once, each numbered and labelled, counted in on a rhythm) and the "4 scenes in 4 seconds" content are kept.
- **No veil, no focus beat.** v1's "one clip leads, the others step back" turned three of four clips into dim slabs every second (critics r03–r05). In v2 every clip is always at full colour. The count is carried by the build itself: each beat a new scene appears and gets its number.
- **The number is anchored, the footage moves (v0).** Each number lands at its final place the moment its scene is revealed (0.06 / 0.71 / 1.21 / 1.71 s, 0.26 s into the previous dock, once the region is clear), and the footage then docks under it. Type never travels, so nothing smears across other type (lesson 7), and every frame from 0.1 s has a readable number.
- **Shot length drives the order.** A v0 cell is on screen from its reveal until the takeover covers it at 4.25 s (BR to the end), so the first cell needs ≥ 4.25 s, the second ≥ 3.8, the third ≥ 3.3, the last ≥ 3.55. Only `s004`, `s008`, `s014`, `s016` and `s119` are long enough for the early slots; `s011` (3.63 s) is the only bright, colourful shot that can carry the last cell and the ending. v1's bands need ≥ 3.88 / 4.6 / 3.04 s. No shot is used in both variants.
- **Faces decide the rows.** Bottom-row shots must have a calm top (no face where the number hangs): `s119` and `s011`. Top-row shots must have a calm lower half: `s004` and `s007`.
- **Light, not a flash.** No white overlay anywhere (`impact` flash 0). The payoff light is `Look.expose` on the footage plus a small coloured flare at the cross; blacks stay black.
- **Endings are designed.** v0 hands back to full screen: type blurs out (3.63 s), the BR cell scales 0.5 → 1 over the grid (3.75–4.25) and the last 0.75 s is clean full-frame Vice City. v1 resolves to a letterbox: type blurs out, band 3 wipes off right and band 1 left (power3.in), and Jason's band holds between the two sunset seams with the kicker in the black above.
- **bg "none".** The clips still show (full-frame component), but the black stage, grain, vignette, impact and expose are off; the seams, pools and sunset line stay (they are part of the graphic).
- **Safe zone.** All type inside x 72–936, y 230–1480: v0 numbers 745–905 and 1010–1175, labels ≈ 697 and ≈ 1200, kicker 1436; v1 numbers 442–598 / 882–1038 / 1322–1478, kicker 248.

## v2 round 6 (craft-v2 redesign, this round)
The owner rejected the v1 look (rounds 1–5, critics ~7) as "basic, not professional or clean-looking". This round is a rebuild from scratch; nothing of the v1 visual design is left.
- **Deleted:** the ink-navy backdrop with blurred footage, the rounded 486×640 / 984×424 cards and their shadows, the smoked-glass and paper chips, the Barlow Condensed title, the segmented progress rule, the rolling "2/4" count, the sunset bottom rule and hairline ring, the veil on the clips that don't lead, and every `data-playback-rate` slow-down (v1 played shots at 0.47–0.91x; v2 plays everything at 1.0x).
- **Footage:** old `assets/plates/` crops replaced by the 4K-graded `assets/shots/`. Grid cells use the vertical `-v` shots at exactly 0.5 scale (a downscale, so they stay sharp); stack bands use the `-h` shots at 0.59 × zoom (≤ 0.82, still a downscale). Every in-point was checked for hidden cuts with scene detection; `s119` starts at 0.07 because its first frame differs.
- **Casting decisions made while building:**
  - v0 TL was `s008` Jason driving; two Jason shots side by side in the top row read as one subject, so TL became `s004` (palm-shirt man) and `s008` moved to v1's middle band (it was `s004-h` there), so the variants share no shot.
  - Bottom-row cells need a calm top for the hanging numbers: `s119` (street) and `s011` (sky), not `s007` (face high in frame), which moved to the top row.
  - `s119`'s gameplay reticle sits at the frame centre, which landed exactly on the kicker (y ≈ 1440). The cell now zooms 1.2 from its top edge, so the reticle drops to y ≈ 1540, below the safe line.
  - `s012`'s bright palm sky made "03 / BEACH GYM" low-contrast; band 3's pool got a second, linear valley from the seam.
- **Numbers land on reveal, not on dock (v0).** In the first build the numbers landed when a cell finished docking, so the first 0.7 s was a bare clip with only the kicker. Now "1 / THE PICKUP" reads from 0.1 s, and each later number lands 0.26 s into the previous dock, when its region is clear (landing at reveal + 0.06 put "3" over the outgoing shot).
- **Legibility without boxes:** panel-local valley gradients either side of the horizontal seam, a soft radial pool behind each grid number (600×520, 46 % at the centre, fades in with the number), and a lift shadow. Labels are Archivo wide 700 (600 failed contrast on Jason's sunlit back).
- **Checks:** type is parked at `autoAlpha: 0` until its reveal starts, which cleared the `text_occluded` and contrast errors that the masked, parked glyphs caused (they were measured against the scrim before they appeared). Archivo is also declared inline so the renderer's static font guard resolves it. `data-layout-allow-overflow` marks the masked reveal containers (the mask padding is intentional).
