# Decisions: 05 GTA 5 vs GTA 6 split

## v2 concept (craft v2, updated round 10)
- **The one idea:** "Same shot, thirteen years apart." Each split is a like-for-like pair from the two trailers, and both sides cut on the same frame: red convertible with red convertible, Vinewood sign with The Keys sign, the Los Santos skyline with Vice City from the sky, sunset with sunset. **The seam between the eras is the light source**, and **the type tells the upgrade**: a smaller 86%-white "GTA V" and a big "GTA VI" in the sunset gradient.
- **Shots and cuts.** v0 Stacked: g33 oil-pump sunset / s084 swamp sunset (0–0.867), **cut + payoff at 0.867** (frame 26) to g07 red convertible / s083 Lucia and Jason in the red convertible, cut at 2.133 to g39 Vinewood / s074 The Keys, cut at 3.167 (with a 0.15 flash) to g16 Los Santos skyline / s011 Vice City highway (in-point 0, held to 5.9; the type exits at 5.0 as the airliner arrives). Every later cut is a beat: the glint re-runs the seam and the place labels swap. v1 Diagonal: g16 skyline full frame, graded contrast 1.22 / saturate 1.1 (0–0.45), **blade reveal + payoff at 0.45** onto s135 skydiving over Vice City (in-point 0: she is on the edge as the blade lands, steps off under the hit and is spread-eagle by the hero frame), cut at 2.05 (smaller glint up the blade, place swap VICE CITY → LEONIDA) to g33 Los Santos skyline at dusk (shrunk into the wedge) / s027 sunset dock framed 200 px low so its lights and sculpted cloud edge run under the blade (both at 0.68x), **takeover at 3.85**: the blade wipes GTA V off the frame, s027 rises back into frame with it, and the GTA VI title glides to a centred end card that lands on its own cut at 4.6 (s026, Lucia and Jason on the pier at golden hour), then blurs out at 5.5.
- **Where the type sits.** v0: the names hug the seam at y=860. 16:9 shots put sky in the top third and ground in the bottom third, so GTA V sits on the dark lower edge of the top shot and GTA VI hangs in the sky at the top of the bottom shot. Faces sit away from the seam (Lucia's face is at y≈1100–1420). A soft valley of shadow either side of the seam carries legibility. v1: the names hug the blade too, with the labels on the outside: GTA V top-left in the sky, GTA VI bottom-right (right-aligned to x=924, label under the name, bottom at 1474). The blade parks high, (0,860) to (1080,220), so GTA VI owns two thirds of the frame and the diver fits between the blade and the name.
- **Hero size and face.** Archivo wide 800 caps. GTA VI is fitted to 744 px (86% of the safe width, about 162 px type) with the Leonida sunset fill and a sheen. GTA V is 0.7x that (about 113 px, 86% white, soft bloom), so the step up reads at 0.6 s on a phone. Labels are Archivo wide 26 px tracked caps ("2013 · LOS SANTOS"). Three sizes, one family.
- **Payoff.** v0 at 0.867 s, on the cut: the seam flares (a specular glint runs its length in 0.35 s expo.out, then a warm #FFE6C8 bloom at .55 decays to .2 over 0.8 s), GTA VI drops out of the seam char by char, its half takes a 0.5 exposure flash, shake + punch-zoom, RGB split, the year rolls 2013→2026 (0.45 s power2.out, digits blurred until it lands), then a sheen. v1 at 0.45 s: the blade slashes up with motion blur, flash + shake, GTA VI drops in under it (0.55), and as the blade parks (0.67 s) the same glint + warm bloom runs up its length. Hero frame: 1.6 s (both titles landed, before either sheen).

## Key decisions (v2)
- **v1's chips and cards are gone.** The paper/navy and smoked-glass era chips scored 7–8 with the old critics but are exactly what the owner called basic: a small dark UI object on soft footage. The names are now title type on the image, and legibility comes from placement plus a gradient scrim.
- **Seam at y=860 (kept from v1).** It is the centre of the 230–1480 safe zone, so both halves show the same visible height above the caption UI. The push on the footage is anchored on the seam (`transformOrigin` at 44.8%), so both halves grow away from it and the line never moves.
- **Sequential reveal in v0.** GTA V reads alone for the first 1.2 s while the bottom half plays unlabelled; GTA VI lands on the cut. This is deliberate: it makes "GTA VI" the punchline, and the hit sits on a cut, as DESIGN.md asks. Every element reads within 0.6 s of appearing.
- **Like-for-like pairs over long holds.** The r05 critic's biggest gap was "the demo never compares like with like" and "v1's GTA V wedge is empty sky". Every pair now matches subjects, and the v1 GTA V framings put the subject in the upper-left wedge (round 7: g01 focus 62% puts the lifeguard flag in the wedge, beside the GTA V name). A pair whose GTA V subject sits low in frame can't work in the diagonal (see round 7, g05).
- **v1 geometry (round 7).** The blade runs from (0,860) to (1080,220) when parked (30.6°). That gives GTA VI two thirds of the frame, which is the argument, and leaves a 560–1300 px band at the centre for the diver between the blade and the GTA VI name. The GTA VI footage no longer parks low (`parkB` is gone); each shot sets its own `y`. The GTA V layer gives way 40 px as the blade passes.
- **v1 ends on a takeover and an end card** (round 8: it now blurs out at 5.5 s instead of holding to the last frame). The blade sweeps on through the top-left (power3.inOut, 0.62 s) with motion blur and the one light leak. GTA V's name lives inside its own wedge (`#clipA`, clipped by the same polygon as the blade), so the blade literally wipes GTA V and its name off the frame. Meanwhile the GTA VI lockup rises from the corner to a centred end card (label slides to centre under it), a spot scrim fades in behind it, and a sheen lands as it arrives. Round 10: the takeover is at 3.85 s and the card lands on its own cut (4.6 s, s026), so the end card is not one held shot; it blurs out at 5.5 s. The GTA V layer's last shot stops at 4.3 s, once it is fully covered.
- **Footage resolution.** v0 uses `-h` shots in 1080×860 and 1080×1060 panels, which are downscaled (0.80x and 0.98x), so even the 1080p GTA V trailer shots are sharp. v1 needs full-height GTA V, which means a 1.78x upscale of a 1080p source. That softness is inherent to the 2013 trailer and reads as the "old" side; the GTA VI side uses the 4K-derived `-v` shots.
- **Gentle slow-mo by frame repeat, not interpolation (round 10).** The r09 0.43x motion-interpolated s027 smeared the water (critic r09), so it is gone. The v1 sunset pair (g33 and s027) plays at 0.68x through `data-playback-rate` (CONFIG `rate`): plain frame repeats, above DESIGN.md's 0.6x judder floor, so the dock shot only has to carry 2.05–4.6 s and the end card gets its own shot. 0.68 rather than 0.7 so g33 keeps a safe margin: it must run until the wipe covers it (4.3 s), i.e. 1.53 s of its 1.633 s.
- **Lint info.** `check` passes: 0 errors. Lint has one advisory warning, `composition_file_too_large` (472 counted lines; it was there in r06 too). The project is kept as one file so CONFIG drives both variants. Layout reports `text_box_overflow` info for chars during the masked reveals and exits (they are translated out of their masks on purpose), with 0 layout warnings, and 34/34 text checks pass WCAG AA.
- **Kept from v1 (rounds 1–5):** 1x playback everywhere except the deliberate pre-slow, both halves cutting on the same frame, no shot replaying footage within a loop, no ESRB/credit frames (every in-point is checked against the shot's own cuts; g28 was rejected because it hides a cut at 1.57 s), and the 2013→2026 year roll (now on the label, `Look.count`).
