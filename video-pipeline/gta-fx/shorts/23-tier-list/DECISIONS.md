# Decisions: 23 Tier list

## Round 1
- **The format follows the TierMaker tier list the top ranking shorts use, made more premium.** The usual red/orange/yellow/green rainbow tier tiles are dropped (DESIGN.md: one accent, no rainbow). Every tier letter is white Barlow Condensed 900. Only the **S** letter carries the Leonida sunset gradient, so the eye goes to the top tier and the frame has one accent.
- **Items are typographic white cards, not cover art.** We can't use official key art (no-trademarks rule). Each card has a Barlow Condensed 800 caps name and a JetBrains Mono second line (a year or a surname). White on smoke or navy stays readable at 360×640.
- **The type follows the game-menu-panel reference** (research/gta6/refs/game-menu-panel/W9oFRFaMo9U-32): a condensed caps header, rows split by hairlines, and the selected row shown as a **white→transparent gradient bar**. That bar is the "verdict" highlight in both variants: v0 flashes it when an item lands, and v1 lights it before the item flies in.
- **v0 Tier rows:** separate smoked-glass strips (r 28, blur 20, 1 px inner highlight) and a straight drop-in (expo.out, 0.36 s) every 0.5 s in release order (1997→2013), so where each game lands is the suspense. The content is the real game list and years from FACTS.md. The ranking is the creator's opinion ("My tier list").
- **v1 Tier reveal:** one solid ink-navy (#1F202B) menu board. Each name first lands on a stage at 1.9× scale under the title. Its tier row lights, then the card files into its slot (power3.inOut, 0.36 s). "Comment yours" pops on the same stage at the end, so the empty band between the title and the board becomes the payoff spot. Names are real characters from Rockstar's site (FACTS.md). Real Dimez's second line is "Bae-Luxe & Roxy". No roles were invented.
- **Sizes:** three sizes: the title and tier letters at 92–104 px, card names and the CTA at 44 px, and labels and second lines at 26 px. Everything sits inside x 72–936, y 246–1416.
- **Legibility:** a top-to-bottom dim gradient (`CONFIG.dim` 0.42) is kept even in the transparent overlay, so the white header reads on bright clips. The title and kicker use the layered soft shadow, not strokes.
- **Plates:** p02 (suggested) shows the official logo for most of its 8 s, so it isn't used (no-trademarks). v0 uses p06 (Lucia, golden hour). v1 uses p03 (Jason), which suits a cast ranking.
- **Loop:** everything fades out and rises 14 px over 7.12–7.5 s.
- **Hero time:** 5.9 s, the full board with the CTA.
- **Change during the round:** the first capture of v1 left an empty band between the title and the board at the end. The CTA moved from under the board onto the reveal stage.

## Round 2 (critic: v0 6/10, v1 7/10; gap = "seven white web-cards outshout the accent, ignore the menu language")
- **Chips now use the game-menu language** (research/gta6/refs/game-menu-panel/W9oFRFaMo9U-32). At rest a chip is a quiet menu row: `rgba(255,255,255,.07)` fill, a 1 px `rgba(255,255,255,.12)` inner hairline, a white Barlow 800 44 px name, and a white 55 % subline. **Only the chip that just landed** carries the reference's selected bar (`linear-gradient(90deg,#fff 0%,#fff 58%,rgba(255,255,255,.28) 100%)`, ink text). The bar passes to the next chip on each drop (0.24 s fade on the old one). Both demos end on an S pick, so the S tier keeps the bar on the end frame. So each beat has one bright object, and the sunset S stays the only colour.
- **v0 is now one board** (the critic named the footage tint and empty slabs as a defect). The four floating slabs are gone. It is one smoked-glass board at `rgba(14,14,20,.80)` with blur, rows split by `.10` hairlines and no gaps. v1 keeps its solid ink-navy board, so the two variants still differ in material and in motion.
- **v1 order fixed:** the reveal stage moved from between the title and the board to **under** the board (board top 430, stage centre y 1180). The frame now always reads title → board → stage/CTA, and "Comment yours" lands right under the ranked board (board bottom + 48 px) in both variants. Nothing has to move at the end, and the dead band from round 1 is gone. v1 chips have an opaque base (`rgb(47,48,58)`, which equals .07 white over the navy), so the big stage card reads on any clip.
- **The CTA is no longer a bright box.** It is the same smoked material as the board (hairline and top highlight) with white Inter 800 44 px type and a white icon. The gloss sweep was removed, which also clears round 1's layout-overflow warnings.
- **Type discipline:** 3 sizes and 2 families. Title and tier letters are 92 px, names and the CTA 44 px, and the kicker and sublines 24 px. The JetBrains Mono sublines became Inter 600 24 px, tracked .14em, with tabular figures, so surnames no longer read as code.
- **Defects fixed:**
  - The dash fragments on chip edges are gone. Chips no longer use `overflow:hidden`, they use `backface-visibility:hidden`, their motion tweens use `force3D:false`, and `clearProps:'transform'` runs once each chip lands. Checked at 2× zoom on both heroes.
  - The flash on consecutive same-row drops is no longer swallowed. The row-flash fade-out is now 0.26 s, so it ends before the next drop's flash in the same row. The check no longer warns about a tween tail.
  - "Every GTA, ranked" became **"Every main GTA, ranked"**, because the list leaves out London 1969, both Stories games and Chinatown Wars.
- Row flash softened to `.16 → .06 → 0` so it supports the selected chip instead of competing with it. Timing: v0 step 0.48 s (CTA 4.3 s), v1 step 0.74 s (CTA 5.3 s).
- **Kept from round 1 (deliberate, no defect named):** plates p06 and p03 (p02 shows the official logo), the S-only sunset accent, the real game list and cast from FACTS.md, and the v1 stage → row reveal.
- **Hero time:** 6.2 s, the full board with the CTA.

## Round 3 (critic: v0 6/10, v1 7/10; gap = "the highlight chip turns grey over the dark board, and the final S pick gets no payoff")
- **[defect] Any number of items per tier.** Round 2 hard-coded 2 slots per row, so a 3rd item in a tier landed at x 936–1269, off the canvas. Now `cols` = the fullest tier, clamped to 2–4, and `chipW = floor((864 − 150 − 16·(cols+1)) / cols)`. Chip side padding shrinks with the column count (24/20/16). A tier with more than 4 items wraps onto extra lines, and only that row grows by `chipH + 16`. If a long list would push the board plus its footer past y 1480, rows and chips shrink together (down to 70 %). Names fit down to a 30 px floor and then wrap to 2 lines. Sublines never go under 24 px: tracking tightens from .14em to .02em, then the text ellipsizes. Tested with 3 items in S and 5 in A (4 columns, A wraps): the board's right edge is at 920 and its bottom at 1388.
- **The selected bar is opaque.** `--selected` is now `linear-gradient(90deg,#FFF 0%,#FFF 70%,#D6D6DC 100%)`, so the dark board never shows through the chip and its right end can't go grey. It still falls off to the right like the reference menu bar (game-menu-panel/W9oFRFaMo9U-32), but stays solid.
- **Deselect is a jump, not a dissolve.** The old chip's bar fades over 0.1 s with power4.out, starting on a frame boundary, and the name and subline snap to white 0.02 s in. That leaves one in-between frame at about 20 % (close to the resting chip colour), and no muddy grey chip. New chips also get a separate fast opacity ramp (0.08 s in v0, 0.06 s for the v1 stage card) instead of fading with the 0.3–0.36 s move, so no white card spends several frames half-transparent. Item beats are snapped to the 30 fps grid. The v0 drop distance went from 96 to 64 px, so the first visible frame of a drop is already inside its own row, not the row above.
- **The final pick gets a payoff:** a 1 → 1.05 → 1 punch (0.09 s up, 0.19 s down), a white lock-in ring that grows 14 px and fades over 0.46 s, and a 1.2× kick on the tier letter. Its row highlight stays lit at 0.5 instead of fading. The critic suggested .22, but the row flash itself is only .16 white at full opacity, so .22 of it could not be seen. The CTA comes 0.45 s after the landing. Timing: v0 step 0.52 and CTA 4.55, v1 CTA 5.57.
- **The CTA is the board's own footer row.** The web pill and its chat-bubble icon are gone. At the CTA beat the board grows 96 px and shows a footer row: a hairline above, a faint `.035` white fill, and "COMMENT YOURS" in Barlow Condensed 800 44 px, tracked .04em, centred. It uses the same material and width as the board (864), so it reads as part of the menu.
- **v1 flight path in 2 segments.** The card first rises straight to its target row's centre while shrinking from 1.9× to 1.4× (0.22 s, power3.out). Then it slides across into its slot and settles to 1× (0.18 s, power3.inOut). Passing the lower rows now takes about 3 frames, and the card visibly arrives in its own lit row before it moves sideways.
- **Ghost slots.** Every slot starts as an empty outline (`inset 0 0 0 1.5px rgba(255,255,255,.10)`) with a centred "?" in Barlow 800 44 px at .25. A chip landing fades its ghost out. The ones left at the end (B in v0; B and C in v1) balance the board. During the hold they breathe (.25 → .55) to point at "Comment yours", which also gives the end hold some life now that the icon wiggle is gone.
- **Flatter board:** the board radius went from 36 to 28 (DESIGN radii 20/28/36), a small step toward Rockstar's flat panel. Chips stay at 20.
- **Kept (deliberate, no defect named):** the two materials (smoked glass in v0, ink navy in v1), the sunset S as the only colour, the one-bright-object-per-beat rule, plates p06/p03 (p02 shows the official logo), the real game list and cast, chronological drops in v0, and stage → row in v1.
- `data-layout-allow-overflow` on the footer (it is hidden below the board until it grows) and `data-layout-allow-occlusion` on the ghosts (a landing chip covers its ghost while it fades). Both are intentional, and the layout check now has 0 warnings. The remaining lint warning (`#ov` nested structure) comes from the template.
- **Hero time:** 6.2 s, the full board with the footer CTA and ghost slots.
