## v2 concept (current as of round 10)
- **The one idea: slam, then dock.** On every real cut the clip number lands as a huge wide-type numeral (Archivo 125%, 800). This is the chapter beat. After a hold it docks out of the way, so the clip plays clean under a small persistent top tracker. The cut to the last clip is the payoff, and it lands on the numeral itself: the "10" rolls in already lit in the Leonida sunset on a crisp flash frame and an exposure push, takes one specular sheen that swells its bloom as it crosses, then docks. As the tenth slot fills, the whole tracker sweeps to the sunset (40 ms stagger) with a glint, then blurs out: the reward for watching to the end. The counting numerals (07–09) are a pearl fill lit from above with a soft white bloom and a quick sheen on each slam. Until the completion beat the tracker stays white with only the current slot lit, so there is one accent per frame.
- **Shots and cuts (8.0 s).** Both edits are full-bleed `-v` shots, each with its own slow push (1.0 → 1.055) and a short white flash plus a small exposure push on each regular cut.

## What carries over from v1 (rounds 1–5)
- **The job and the mechanic.** It's a persistent compilation tracker that **advances on real cuts only**, never on a timer (r03 defect). The current slot fills through its clip, so the tracker is never static.
- **The odometer.** There's one rolling column per digit, and a column only gets a new row when its digit changes. The window is the numeral's measured ink box (canvas `measureText` plus a baseline probe) with a feather just outside it, so a rolling digit fades out but the resting digit is never faded (r04 and r05). The row pitch is the window plus 0.16 em, so a neighbour is never in view at rest (r05 crescent fix). The type shadow or glow is a `drop-shadow` filter on a wrapper outside the mask (r04).
- **State changes are hard swaps** (zero-duration tweens 2 ms before the cut), so the cut frame itself shows the new state (r01 and r04).
- **Sequenced title swap.** The old line is fully out and hidden before the new one starts rising, so they never share a frame (r05 defect).
- **The exit finishes before the last frame.** Since r10 it's a staggered blur-out from 7.45 s, fully gone by 7.9 s in the 8.0 s comp (r05 ghost defect).
- **Numbers on the dots** (r03 defect). v1 showed 01–10 under the stations until r06. In r07 only the current dot carried its index. Since r08 all ten are numbered again: past 86% white, upcoming 40%, current in the sunset. In r07 the rail read as a carousel pager (r07 critique). They were 24 px until r09 and are 30 px since r10, so they still read at phone size.
- Every tween is a `fromTo` with `immediateRender: false`, start states are set at build, and there's no randomness. Since r10 every hit that jumps to a state and decays back (flashes, the exposure push, pings, slam punches) is a `pulse`: a hard step from the resting state first, so a backward seek lands on the resting state, not on the hit.

## What v1 got wrong (owner: "basic, not professional or clean")
- It was a small HUD and a smoked capsule floating over soft 1080p plates. The type was 132 px Barlow and 28 px labels, there was no finishing layer and no payoff light. At phone size it read as a web widget.
- v2 removes every card. The counter is now the title of the frame, and the footage is graded 4K cut to the counter's beats.
