# Blurred fill frame: decisions

## v2 concept
- **One idea: an ambient screen.** The 16:9 shot plays sharp as a lit screen, and the same shot, blown up and blurred, glows around it like light spilling off a screen into a dark room. An edge spill (the shot's own edges stretched past the frame and blurred) makes the light visibly bleed out of the frame. Every cut re-colours the whole 9:16 frame, so the colour arc of the edit (day blue to golden hour) becomes the design.
- **Shots and cuts (`assets/shots/*-h.mp4`).** v0 (since r10): `s011` Vice City highway 0-2.1, `s064` jetski 2.1-3.2 (in 0.5: the close pass), `s027` sunset dock 3.2-4.1 (a 0.9 s quick cut only), `s026` sunset pier 4.1-5.63, `s108` balcony sunset 5.63-6.6 (Launch Trailer, in 0.2). The first four are Trailer 2. v1: `s084` swamp sunset 0-1.4 (Launch Trailer), `s039` blue club 1.4-2.93 (in 1.1: one continuous orbit between the trailer's own cuts at 1.067 and 2.667), `s036` beach bar 2.93-4.3, `s018` Lucia at the gate 4.3-6.6. Cuts are frame-aligned, no shot runs past its length, and no shot carries a baked-in trailer cut.
- **Where the type sits.** v0 (since r08) puts its title in the lit room above an inset screen and its source line just below it. Since r09 the screen is 900x506 (x 54-954, y 707-1213), the title glyphs sit at y 531-624 and the credit at y 1268, all centred on the safe-zone axis x 504 like the screen, so nothing sits under the action rail. The room is the image's own light, so the type is lit by the shot, and it never touches the picture. v1 puts an 864x486 frame on the text grid (x 72-936, y 532-1018, clear of the action rail), one slate row above it (source + live timecode left, handle right, y 474) and the caption tight under it (y 1052-1330), inside the light the frame throws downward.
- **Hero size and face.** v0 (r09): "LEONIDA" in Archivo wide 900 at 134 px, tracking .05em (ink x 91-916, 825 px, 95 % of the safe width and 92 % of the screen), in Rockstar's date-card style: pure white, a hard 3x2 px `#EE08F2` offset and a soft white and magenta bloom. The kicker is Archivo wide 24 px white with a 1.5 px offset; the credit is Archivo wide 24 px at 88 %, 5.6:1. v1 (r08): a setup line "Every frame" in Inter 700 at 112 px, 86 % white, over the payoff line "is unreal." in Inter 800 at 184 px, -0.05em (737 px, 85 % of the 864 px column), with "unreal." in sunset; the slate is Inter 700 at 27 px. One family per variant.
- **Payoff hit.** v0 at **2.1 s** on the cut into the jetski's close pass (since r08): "LEONIDA" becomes a window onto the jetski shot itself (video-in-type). Since r09 the crop starts 2.3x close on the green hull and pulls back over 0.5 s, graded darker and denser than the white title, with a 1.5 px inner white edge. A 2-frame light hit (exposure push, blacks held), a shake with a punch-in and a white bloom swell land with it. After 0.62 s, the white wipes back right to left, chasing the jetski out of frame (done by 2.98 s), with a bloom swell. A warm leak carries the 3.2 s cut into golden hour, and a smaller bloom swell marks the 5.63 s cut into the last shot (r10). v1 at **1.4 s** on the cut from the swamp sunset into the club: "is unreal." slams in as one unit (scale 1.25 to 1, blur 16 to 0, anchored left, 1.25-1.5 s since r10, so it is sharp on the cut) with the light hit, shake and a bloom swell on the sunset word. A 5 px RGB split runs on that line only, at 1.52 s for 0.15 s, then a sheen. A leak and a second sheen carry the cut to Lucia at 4.3 s.

## Key decisions (v2)
- **Two real styles.** v0 "Title + clip" is a *centred title card around an inset screen* (r08; r04-r07 used a full-bleed 1080x608 band): 900x506 at x 54 y 707 since r09 (r08: 960x540 at x 60 y 690), radius 6, a 1 px lit top edge and a contact shadow, with the room lit on all four sides, a floor reflection and the video-in-type payoff. It opens from a 135 px slit of footage (k .25, expo.out over 0.5 s) and closes back to that slit on the exit, so the last frame matches frame 0. v1 "Clip + caption" is *framed*: an inset 864x486 frame (radius 8 since r07, on the x 72-936 text grid since r06) with a lit top edge, a contact shadow and a body shadow. It resolves out of its own glow (blur 18 to 0) and dissolves back into it on the exit. The r03 critic called that dissolve "lovely" and asked for it on the entrance too.
- **Three copies of each shot, frame-locked (four in v0).** These are the glow (cover-scaled, drawn at 1/4 size, `blur(16px)` then x4, about 64 px), the spill (screen-sized, stretched 1.12x1.5 in v0 and 1.1x1.42 in v1, blurred about 30 px, mask-faded, screen-blended) and the sharp screen. v0 also has a mirrored floor reflection copy, plus one copy of the hit shot inside the title (`#t0b`). All copies share src and timing. `duplicate_media_discovery_risk` is expected: it is how the layers stay in sync. Inactive-variant videos sit in containers with `display:none`.
- **The room falloff and the floor.** The glow is saturated (`brightness .70`, `saturate 1.65` by default since r07, per-shot overrides) next to the screen and falls off toward the top edge. Below the clip it is graded as coloured shadow (r07: a soft-light ramp plus a deep violet tint; r08: navy and eased in v0), never left as raw blur. Since r08, a per-shot multiply `tint` turns white sky or spray in the glow into the scene's colour. That avoids the earlier failures: r02's "orange-grey fog", r03's "charcoal template" and r06's "ungraded mud". Two soft radial scrims sit only behind v0's title and source line, and one sits behind v1's caption; v1's slate row gets a per-shot scrim on light shots. There are no boxes.
- **No generic card.** The only object is the footage itself. The Follow pill went in r05 (app chrome); the avatar-and-handle byline went in r07 (template part). The handle is now a label at the right end of the slate row.
- **Live source timecode (kept from v1).** "ROCKSTAR GAMES · TRAILER 2 · 2:20" (v0) and "TRAILER 2 · 1:40" (v1) show each shot's real position in Trailer 2 and tick with the footage. They jump on every cut, using the `src` in-points from `gta6/_shared/shots/shots.json`. One `Look.drive` over the whole piece computes them, along with v0's aperture height and spill scale, from absolute time, so every seek is exact. The label flips on the cut frame (half-frame tolerance).
- **Content.** "Welcome to Leonida" is a plain hook, not a claim. Leonida is the state from FACTS.md, and every shot is from Trailer 2 (VQRLujxTm3c). "Every frame is unreal." is an opinion in the channel's own voice, not "this shot", because the clip cuts (the r03 critic flagged a caption about one shot over a plate that cut). There are no logos.
- **bg variable.** This is a full-frame component, so footage always shows. `bg:"none"` turns off the finishing stack, the leak and the flash (grain, vignette, leak, flash); shake and RGB split stay.

## v1 history (r01-r03, superseded)

### Concept
One object made with care: a 16:9 clip as a rounded card (936 x 527, radius 28) centred over a blurred, enlarged, still-bright copy of the same clip, so the whole 9:16 frame glows with the shot's colour. This is the vertical re-frame top creators use to put landscape trailer or gameplay footage into a 9:16 short (refs: `blurred-side-fill-vertical-clip`, `framed-inset-blur-bg`, `social-post-overlay-blur-fill`). Those refs are flat: a hard-edged inset, a muddy fill, a Follow pill pasted on. Ours adds card material (contact shadow + large soft shadow, 1 px inner highlight), a drifting fill, a slow push on the framed clip and one sunset accent.

### Key decisions
- **Same clip, two layers.** The fill and the card are two `<video>` elements with the same `src` and timing, so they stay frame-locked. HyperFrames warns `duplicate_media_discovery_risk`. That is expected: the render shows both layers in sync.
- **Cheap, strong blur.** The fill is drawn at 1/4 size, blurred 11 px and scaled x4, which gives a ~44 px blur at a fraction of the cost. The blur bleeds past the frame edges, so there are no dark seams.
- **A bright, coloured fill; text protected locally (r03).** `brightness 0.85, saturate 1.35, blur 44`, a light global shade (top/bottom only) and one soft radial scrim (`rgba(11,11,14,.55)` fading out by 70 %) placed only behind the text block of each variant. The fill is the component's signature: it should glow with the clip (blue sky stays blue, golden hour stays gold), as in Rockstar's social-overlay frame (`QdBZY2fkU-0-42.2`) and the side-fill refs. Measured fill luma outside the text: v0 about 45-120 depending on the shot, v1 50-90 (r02 was 12-48).
- **Fill focus (r03).** `fill.focus` picks which vertical stripe of the 16:9 frame the fill samples (0 left, 0.5 centre, 1 right). A cover-scaled 16:9 clip only shows ~28 % of its width behind a 9:16 frame, so on p06 the centre stripe is Lucia's dark T-shirt. v0 samples the sunlit fence side (`focus 0.8`, `brightness 1.0` as a per-variant override), which keeps the golden-hour glow without lifting the whole grade.
- **No letterbox trim on the sample plates (r02).** The shared plates were re-cut without bars, so `CONFIG.card.trim` is now `0` and the card shows the full 16:9 frame. `trim: 0.1` stays documented for creators using a 2.22:1 letterboxed rip.
- **Card width = frame minus 72 px gutters.** The card is centred (x 72 to 1008). Footage may sit under the right-rail buttons, as all full-frame footage does. All text stays inside x 72 to 936, left-aligned to the card's left edge.
- **Live source timecode.** The timestamp is the clip's real position in Rockstar's Trailer 2 (p06 starts at 60.7 s, p05 at 13.8 s) and ticks with the footage. It is driven by a getter/setter proxy tween, so it is correct on every seek. Both variants set it in Inter 600 with tabular figures (r03: the v0 JetBrains Mono timecode is gone).
- **Photo avatar (r03).** `CONFIG.avatar` is a square photo shown as a disc with a 1 px `rgba(255,255,255,.18)` ring drawn above the photo. The sample is a fan-made crop (sun and lifeguard-tower silhouette) from the g02 beach plate, `assets/img/avatar.jpg`. Leave it empty to fall back to initials.
- **6.6 s, not 7 s (r03).** The p05 plate hard-cuts to a new shot at 6.67 s. Both variants now end at 6.6 s (exit 6.16 s), so no new shot can flash inside the exit fade. p06 cuts at 2.4 s and 5.5 s, both inside the hold.
- **One accent at a time:** the Leonida sunset gradient wipes into the highlighted phrase only. Everything else is white or smoke. The v1 Follow pill's gold ring is taken straight from Rockstar's own post UI. From r03 it never shares the screen with the sunset: the gold pill is tapped at 1.25 s and drops to a white hairline by 1.52 s, and only then (1.55 s) the sunset sweeps into "unreal.".
- **Real content only.** Both clips are from Rockstar's Trailer 2 (VQRLujxTm3c). The v0 title describes what is on screen (Lucia walking out of the gate, Jason waiting in the lot). The v1 caption ("The detail in this shot is unreal.") is an opinion in the channel's own voice; the chip carries the source, so "Trailer 2" appears once per frame.
